## Scope
Operate. Surfaces: client app (`/`, phone first, desktop rail layout) and staff workspace (`/staff/`, desktop first, three panes). Task: keep a family's records, see dynamics, talk to the assistant (client); clear the inbox, enter lab values, run requests (staff).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The family's story is one thread. A running stitch (dashed indigo thread) is the single signature move: it carries a person's records through time on the client timeline and divides sections. It refuses the category default of a grey SaaS dashboard with card grids and a medical-green skin.

OWN-WORLD: Indigo-dyed linen. Committed indigo shell (rail, header, active states), cool pale linen-grey ground, white surfaces without nesting, ink-indigo text. Single accent family: indigo. Red only for values outside the lab range and for errors. Onest for all type, tabular numerals, drawn SVG icons in one stroke weight, running-stitch dividers and one stitched reference band on charts.

STORY: The visitor sees at once whose records these are, what changed, what is outside the lab range, and what the assistant is doing. Staff see who is waiting, for how long, and what each client needs, without hunting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: wordmark and user menu, profile switcher with initials, person header (name, age, consent state), then the stitched timeline of the latest events with the out-of-range block above it, bottom tab bar. Desktop: 264px indigo rail with nav and profiles; content in a wide column with timeline left and attention panel right. Staff: slim indigo rail, inbox list, conversation, client context panel; primary action is the reply box.

FORM: Candidate 6 of my grounded list (Indigo linen and stitch); seed key 58264c1b; degraded roll, no challengers.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
